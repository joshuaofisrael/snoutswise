Worksheet: What does a dog need?

A one page worksheet for kindergarten to grade 2 about what dogs need to live and grow, plus a safe or not safe food check. Answer key below the worksheet.

Name: ____________________   Date: __________

Part 1. Circle the things every dog needs to live and grow.

  1. Food    2. Water    3. A video game    4. A safe place to rest    5. A hat

Part 2. Draw a line from the dog to what it needs.

Thirsty dog  →  ______________     Hungry dog  →  ______________     Sleepy dog  →  ______________

Word bank: water, a bed, dog food

Part 3. Safe or not safe? Write S for safe or N for not safe.

___ Grapes    ___ Plain carrot    ___ Chocolate    ___ Plain apple slice (no seeds)    ___ Onion

Part 4. Draw a dog and its water bowl.

Answer key (for teachers)

  1. Part 1: food, water and a safe place to rest. All animals need food in order to live and grow, and all living things need water (NGSS K-LS1-1).
  2. Part 2: thirsty dog: water; hungry dog: dog food; sleepy dog: a bed.
  3. Part 3: grapes N, plain carrot S, chocolate N, plain apple slice without seeds S, onion N. The ASPCA lists grapes, chocolate and onions as foods to keep away from pets; the American Kennel Club lists plain carrots and apple slices (without seeds or core) as safe treats in moderation.
  4. Part 4: any drawing showing a dog with water.

Want a game version? Play Snack or Nope? on the class screen.

NGSS connection

  • K-LS1-1: Use observations to describe patterns of what plants and animals (including humans) need to survive.
