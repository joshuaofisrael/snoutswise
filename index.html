Quick facts

  • A healthy adult dog's average body temperature is about 101 to 102.5°F (38.3 to 39.2°C), and its resting breathing rate is about 18 to 34 breaths a minute (more on normal vital signs).
  • Grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, chocolate, macadamia nuts and the sweetener xylitol are all on the ASPCA's list of foods to keep away from dogs (see the full table).
  • The American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior calls the first three months of life the primary window for puppy socialization (how to use it safely).
  • In a 14 year study of Labrador retrievers, dogs fed 25 percent less than their littermates lived a median 1.8 years longer (how to check your dog's weight).
