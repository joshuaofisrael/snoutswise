Image policy

How SnoutsWise pictures are made: AI generated illustrations that say so in their own caption, and licensed real photographs credited on our photo credits page.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Two kinds of pictures

SnoutsWise uses two kinds of pictures, and the caption under every one says which kind it is.

AI generated images

Most pictures on the site, mainly on blog posts, are AI generated images made for SnoutsWise with an AI image tool. They are illustrations in a photo style, not photographs of a real dog, a real person or a real event. No AI image on the site shows a real person, a real brand, a real place or any text. Each one carries a caption that says it is an AI generated image, so it is never mistaken for a real news photo.

Licensed real photographs

Some pages, mainly the breed, care, health, training, behavior, feeding and age calculator pages, use real photographs instead. Every one of these is a public domain or Creative Commons licensed photo from Wikimedia Commons. We open each photo's own file page on Wikimedia Commons and check its licence before using it, and we only use photos licensed CC BY, CC BY SA or public domain, never a No Derivatives or Non Commercial licence. We crop and resize these photos and convert them to WebP; our edited versions carry the same licence as the original. Each photographer, source and licence is listed, with a credit, on our photo credits page.

Illustrations and game art

The logo, the paw and bone doodles, the icons and the artwork in our games are original drawings made for SnoutsWise. They are not photographs and not AI generated images.

Questions about an image

If you have a question about where a picture came from, or you believe one is credited incorrectly, see the full list on our photo credits page or get in touch through the contact page.
