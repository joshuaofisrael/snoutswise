Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Who we are

Dog Field Guide is operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Browsing. The site has no user accounts and sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security and operation; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Analytics. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy focused, cookieless service, to count page views in aggregate. It does not use cookies or build profiles of individual visitors. If and when it is enabled, it loads a small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our email inbox. We use them only to read and reply to your message. Email us at any time to ask us to delete your message.
  • Email. If you email us directly, we keep your message and address only as long as needed to deal with it.

Advertising and affiliates

The site currently shows no ads and contains no affiliate links. We will update this policy before that changes.

Third party links

Pages link to outside sources such as veterinary manuals and kennel clubs. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Children

The site is general information for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Email us and we will respond.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.

Contact us
