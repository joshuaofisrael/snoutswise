Who we are

SnoutsWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC owns and operates snoutswise.com and is the data controller for personal information collected through it ("we", "us"). Contact: joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect today

  • Hosting server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub's servers may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, for security and to keep the service running. We do not receive these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. Pages load the Fredoka and Nunito fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). To deliver them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. See Google Fonts privacy information and Google's privacy policy.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, your name, email address, chosen topic and message are sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our email inbox, which is a Gmail account provided by Google. We use them only to read and reply to your message.
  • Email. If you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It is cookieless: it sets no cookies, does not use local storage, and does not fingerprint visitors or track them across sites. Cloudflare processes basic request data such as the page URL, referrer, browser type and approximate country to produce aggregate statistics. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Google Analytics 4 (only if you accept)

We also use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google LLC, to understand how visitors use SnoutsWise so we can improve it. Google Analytics does not load until you click Accept in our analytics banner. If you click Decline, or make no choice, it is not loaded and sets no cookies.

What it collects. Usage data such as the pages you view, time on page, how you arrived (for example a search engine or a link) and clicks on the site, plus device data such as browser, operating system, screen size, language and approximate location (city or country) derived from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We see this only as aggregate reports, we do not use it to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies. If you accept, Google Analytics sets first party cookies named _ga and _ga_GRC8LBNEX4, which last up to two years and let it tell repeat visits apart. Your choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage as mtm-analytics-consent.

Consent. We use Google Consent Mode. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics and advertising storage start as denied by default, and Google's consent message described below controls advertising choices. For every visitor, analytics storage stays denied until you accept our banner. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page, which shows the banner again; choosing Decline then removes the Google Analytics cookies.

Opt out. You can also block Google Analytics on every website with the Google Analytics opt out browser add on. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC may show ads on SnoutsWise through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. To learn how Google uses data when you use our site, and how to opt out of personalized advertising, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can also opt out of many third party advertising cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

California residents (CCPA). You can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising through the Google and aboutads.info links above, and by emailing us. We do not otherwise sell personal information.

Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland. Google shows you a consent choice about cookies and personalized ads. You can accept, decline or change your choice there, and ads will respect it. Our own analytics banner covers Google Analytics only.

What we do not use

The site has no user accounts and sets no cookies of its own, apart from the Google Analytics cookies described above if you accept them. Apart from Google AdSense, described above, it uses no advertising networks, and it has no affiliate links or affiliate cookies. If we add any, we will update this policy before they go live.

How we use and share information

We use contact form messages and emails only to respond to you and to improve or correct the site. We do not sell personal information. Advertising through Google AdSense is described in the Advertising section above. The service providers named above (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and FormSubmit) process data on our behalf or as part of delivering their services, under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with them and then delete them. Email us at any time to ask us to delete your message.

Third party links

Pages link to outside sources such as veterinary manuals and kennel clubs. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Children

The site is general information for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. Email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com and we will respond.

Games

Our games save your best score in your own browser (localStorage) so you can try to beat it. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and contains no personal information. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Photos

Photos on the site are stored on our own pages (self hosted), so viewing them does not contact any other service. See photo credits.
