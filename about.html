About Dog Field Guide

Dog Field Guide is a free, independent reference for dog owners, operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. We turn veterinary and kennel club guidance into clear, practical pages and show the sources behind every claim.

Who runs this site

Dog Field Guide is operated by Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. It is an independent publication and is not affiliated with any kennel club, veterinary organization, pet food company or retailer named on the site.

What we cover

Practical, plain English information for dog owners: breed groups, everyday care, health basics, training and behavior, plus tools such as the Can dogs eat this? table and the dog age calculator.

How content is produced

  • Pages are researched and written by the Dog Field Guide editorial team with the help of AI tools, then checked against primary and authoritative sources such as the Merck Veterinary Manual, the American Animal Hospital Association, the American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior, the American Veterinary Medical Association, the ASPCA, the RSPCA, the American Kennel Club, The Royal Kennel Club, university veterinary schools and peer reviewed studies.
  • Every page lists its sources, and facts and figures are attributed to the organization that published them. We do not invent statistics, quotes, experts or personal experiences.
  • We are not veterinarians, and nothing here replaces an examination by a vet who knows your dog. Health and safety pages say so and point to emergency help.
  • Each page shows when it was last updated. We review pages when guidelines change.

Money

The site is free to read. It currently carries no advertising, sponsored content or affiliate links. If that changes, we will say so clearly on the affected pages and in our privacy policy.

Corrections

Spotted an error or an outdated guideline? Please email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com or use the contact form with the page and the source, and we will check and correct it.
