Can dogs learn the names of their toys? A few gifted dogs can, fast

Published 26 October 2026 · Last reviewed 26 October 2026 · By SnoutsWise Editorial Team

Most dogs learn words like sit and walk, but learning the names of objects is different. A 2021 study in Scientific Reports found that two dogs who already knew many toy names could learn a new toy's name after hearing it just four times while playing with their owner. The same test with 20 ordinary family dogs found no sign of learning, which suggests this is a rare talent.

The gifted dogs

Researchers in Hungary tested two dogs that already knew the names of multiple toys. Each dog heard a new toy's name four times and was then tested on whether it had learned which toy the name belonged to.

Play beats puzzles

The dogs met new names in two ways. In one, the owner named the toy while playing with the dog. In the other, the dog had to work out which unfamiliar toy matched a new word by ruling out the toys it already knew. Both dogs succeeded after the social play sessions but not after the exclusion task, so the authors suggest that fast word learning in dogs depends on a social setting.

Memory fades without practice

The dogs still remembered the new names after two minutes, but their memory tended to fade after 10 minutes and after an hour. Four exposures seem to be enough to learn a name, but not enough to keep it for long.

Most dogs could not do it

The team ran the same play-based procedure with 20 typical family dogs. None showed evidence of learning the new names. The authors say future studies should find out whether the skill comes from rare individual talent or from earlier practice with object names.

What this means for owners

  • Do not worry if your dog does not learn toy names. In this study ordinary family dogs did not learn them, so it is not a sign that anything is wrong.
  • If you want to try, keep it playful. The gifted dogs learned best when names came up during play with their owner, which fits the reward based training approach.
  • Head tilts may be part of it. A separate study linked frequent head tilting to dogs that know toy names (see why dogs tilt their heads).

Frequently asked questions

Can dogs learn the names of their toys?

A few can. In a 2021 Scientific Reports study, two dogs that already knew many toy names learned a new one after four exposures during play, but 20 typical family dogs did not.

How do gifted dogs learn toy names best?

In the study, the dogs learned new names when the owner named the toy while playing, not when they had to work it out by ruling out familiar toys.

Do dogs remember new toy names?

The gifted dogs remembered them after two minutes, but memory tended to fade after 10 minutes and one hour, so more practice may be needed.
